# YRBS narrative mapping correction — 2026-10-13

The CSV keeps its existing `question,code,prompt_fragment` interface. Numeric height and weight (Q6/Q7) remain the profile generator's responsibility. Q5 keeps its documented letter-combination representation; this is not an instruction to interpret arbitrary raw CDC race fields as those letters without decoding.

## Substantive corrections

- Q93 is lifetime hallucinogenic drug use, not absence from school due to transportation.
- Q97 is muscle-strengthening exercise, not tooth brushing.
- Q98 is sunburn in the past 12 months, not mental-health appointments.
- Q99 is a household adult trying to meet basic needs, not school safety.
- Q81 is lifetime HIV testing excluding blood-donation tests, not testing in the past year.
- Q59 option 2 means previous sexual intercourse but none in the past three months; its former fragment was malformed.
- Restore explicit non-participation options that were blank, remove the invalid Q87 code 8, and collapse duplicate keys. Blank source responses remain missing, never a negative answer.
- Preserve important time windows, before/after distinctions, food exclusions, the definition of the alcohol threshold, and qualifying clauses. Improve awkward identity, uncertainty and frequency statements without inferring a diagnosis or personality.

`mapping_corrections_20261013.json` records every changed key, including all earlier duplicate fragments. The previous CSV is recoverable from Git history. Sources checked include this repository's `questions_107_converted.json` and the CDC 2023 National YRBS SAS input/format programs, particularly the Q93/Q97/Q98/Q99 variable labels and the Q87 allowed values.

## Validation and effect on previous outputs

Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_yrbs_mapping.py'` from the repository root. Checks cover every categorical question/option, all 31 nonempty Q5 combinations, duplicate keys, nonempty fragments, substantive corrections and two classroom examples. These tests do not substitute for research replication.

Previously generated baseline profiles, memories, conversations and validation results have **not** been regenerated by this correction. Regenerate affected artifacts, including separately rebuilt held-out profiles, before reporting results as based on this corrected mapping. Retain the mapping revision in each run manifest; do not overwrite historical results or attribute them to the new table.
